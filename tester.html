PROXY TESTER

Checks what your browser, your website host, and your proxy can and can't do, then gives you one report to copy. The browser checks work on their own. Enter your proxy's address to test the proxy too.

Using the proxy on your own computer? Enter http://localhost:8080.
If this page is on https, your proxy address must be https too (localhost is the one exception). Tip: add ?sites=https://a.com,https://b.com to this page's address to test your own list.
